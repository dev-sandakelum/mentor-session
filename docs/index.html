How it works

Four simple rules keep it fair for everyone.

1

Pick your top 3

Browse the mentor directory and rank your three favourite mentors in order.

2

Submission order matters

Preferences are processed first-come, first-served — earlier submissions win contested spots.

3

2-mentee cap

Each mentor takes a limited number of mentees, so groups stay small and personal.

4

Random fallback

If all three picks fill up, you're randomly matched with a mentor who still has room.
