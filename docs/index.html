ICT Students' Circle · Mentor Session 2026

Find your senior mentor. Grow together.

The ICTSC Mentor Session connects junior students with experienced seniors for academic guidance, study advice, technical direction and real university experience — matched fairly, transparently and automatically.

1

Pick 3 Ranked Mentors

Every mentee chooses a 1st, 2nd and 3rd priority mentor from the approved senior mentor pool.

2

First Come, First Served

Preferences are processed strictly by server-side submission time — the earlier you submit, the earlier you're matched.

3

Max 2 Mentees / Mentor

Small groups keep mentoring personal. Once a mentor has two mentees, they're marked full.

4

Random Fallback

If all three of your choices fill up, the system fairly assigns you to a mentor with remaining capacity.
